See what a bulk SMS will really cost

One curly apostrophe turns a 1-part SMS into 3. One name with an accent does it for that person only. And a list of 500 numbers is often 460 people. Check before you press Send.

Runs in your browser with the kit's own code. Nothing is uploaded or sent.
